# Movement corrections change accounting inputs, not tax conclusions

## Status

Accepted and implemented through REST and the Effect/Promise SDKs in **#150 — feat(tax): apply audited user overrides to transaction calculations**. The maintainer confirmed this boundary on 2026-09-06. The decisions below include the refinements recorded during delivery.

## Decision

A principal may correct the valuation or supported factual classification of one recorded token movement. The correction preserves provider evidence, applies only to the owning principal, and supplies effective inputs to the accounting engine. Treatment codes remain engine results; users do not write taxable/exempt conclusions or change accounting methods through this capability.

Asset identity and inclusion remain the asset-wide overrides in ADR 0011. A movement correction never changes those decisions, token quantities, occurrence times, ownership, or reconciliation approval. A corrected price cannot unblock an excluded asset or manufacture a missing acquisition, transfer approval, or legally decisive fact.

### Identity and history

The producer records a stable movement identity from its actual source record and explicit component identity. It is independent of a derived leg UUID, the selected economic asset, the price, and the effective classification. A durable movement target survives deletion and recreation of derived rows. Readers follow recorded links under ADR 0012; they never find targets by amounts, sibling rows, or current list positions. A target that disappears or changes structural meaning stays in history and needs attention; the correction is never moved to a replacement movement by guessing.

Price and classification have independent append-only streams per principal and movement target. Reuse ADR 0011's create/replace/withdraw semantics, authenticated actor, required reason, inspected system revision, expected stream leaf, and atomic conflict checks. Reuse the existing source replay scheduling and covering-run rules rather than creating another job system. Asset-wide target semantics are not reused as the identity of a single movement.

Current system facts, the inspected facts retained with each correction, active user input, and effective facts remain separate. Later provider changes do not erase an override. Structural changes that prevent safe application are explicit blockers, not permission to apply the correction elsewhere. Source/principal deletion cannot cascade away correction history.

Acceptance records the current effective economic asset through the movement's recorded asset-decision links, even when the stored leg still names a different system asset. Missing required identity links remain unresolved; a raw asset is not a substitute. A later effective asset change requires attention and replacement to confirm the new facts. Existing history is never rewritten to repair an earlier inspection. This preserves the writer boundary in ADR 0012 ([T08P decision](https://github.com/taxmaxi/taxmaxi/issues/150#issuecomment-5560226827)).

Acceptance also retains the exact pre-correction system valuation facts, their reporting currency, and original event/evidence references. The inspection revision compares their meaning: fact kind, exact value, currency, quote time/source, and stable inspected movement facts. Regenerated IDs alone do not make identical replayed evidence stale. Changed provider consideration or market quotes used by the shared factual loader do; unused consideration that emitted no valuation fact does not. This comparison reuses existing factual eligibility and valuation construction, not a second price policy ([T09P decision](https://github.com/taxmaxi/taxmaxi/issues/150#issuecomment-5560747126)).

### Valuation and calculation

Price input is either a unit price or a total value for the selected movement, never an ambiguous transaction-wide amount. Preserve the input mode, exact amount, currency, and inspected quantity. The input currency is the calculation's reporting currency in this first version; residence and interface locale do not determine it. Foreign-currency entry and conversion are separate work.

Unit-price entry derives the total from the exact price and positive quantity. Total-value entry keeps the entered total authoritative; a repeating unit preview is labelled rounded, half away from zero to 18 fractional places, and is never multiplied back into the total. Exact zero is known user value; missing value remains unknown. Inputs never pass through JavaScript numbers or the legacy eight-decimal leg fiat fields.

For user valuations, the engine retains each lot's remaining total and quantity privately. Partial disposals and custody splits allocate from those remainders, rounded half away from zero to the greater of 18 fractional places and the original total's number of fractional places. Each allocation is subtracted; the final consumed portion receives the exact remainder. Custody destinations retain their allocated total and precision. User-valued disposal proceeds follow the same rule across FIFO matches; inventory shortage leaves unmatched quantity and its share of proceeds unallocated. This conserves an entered total such as EUR 1 for three tokens without reconstructing it from a rounded unit preview. Provider/market-only arithmetic and FIFO selection remain unchanged ([T05 precision decision](https://github.com/taxmaxi/taxmaxi/issues/150#issuecomment-5559367302)).

An applicable active price correction takes precedence over provider consideration and market quotes for that movement. Its evidence remains user supplied: it is never represented as provider-observed consideration or a market quote. The engine consumes a typed user valuation fact without knowing about users, persistence, or override history management. Without a price correction, existing valuation selection is unchanged.

A factual correction changes a supported acquisition or disposition cause while preserving ownership direction and recorded movement structure. Its evidence remains an explicit user assertion, including passive staking; it never becomes provider confirmation. Engine validation and jurisdiction rules still run. Classifying a receipt as an airdrop, gift, or generic staking reward can leave it blocked where the engine needs additional facts. A movement label cannot create an approved transfer between owned sources. Explicit fees keep their fee relationship and may receive a price correction; approved custody movements cannot receive replacement basis or become acquisitions/disposals through a correction.

The existing factual-ledger loader is the application boundary. It reads correction history in the calculation's repeatable-read snapshot, applies effective inputs, and records which correction records and exact effective values the run consumed. Runs with different correction histories have different input revisions even when amounts happen to be equal. A current override is never shown as applied to an older run that did not consume it. Failed application leaves explicit partial/updating/failed status and does not delete history or stop unrelated valid source activity.

An incompatible correction on an eligible event is withheld while valid current system inputs remain, with a typed correction blocker making the run partial. An absent target retains history and attention with no current effective event; a target or historical leg ID must never substitute for a current event ID. Already-withheld activity retains its technical blockers and correction context. Outside-period records remain explicitly not applied ([T07 outcome decision](https://github.com/taxmaxi/taxmaxi/issues/150#issuecomment-5559927499)).

### Current inspection, work, and run coverage

Current inspection and selected calculation inputs have different scopes. Within one repeatable-read transaction, load current facts without a year cutoff once per required inspection currency, and load the selected-year/currency factual snapshot separately. Compare each correction in its own inspected currency: opening another year or currency does not itself make it stale. Resolve whole-transaction and custody eligibility before filtering to a requested target. A malformed current movement remains visible with unavailable inspection and its actual withheld facts; it is distinct from a disappeared target. Strict mutation inspection still rejects facts that cannot satisfy its contract ([T09 read decisions](https://github.com/taxmaxi/taxmaxi/issues/150#issuecomment-5560944140)).

Each stream's current attention, requested replay work, and covering-run result remain separate. Coverage requires that stream's selected leaf, including withdrawal, in the run's immutable captured inputs for the same principal, target, jurisdiction, year, and currency, plus snapshot visibility of the completed requested replay/follow-up rows. Applied status comes from the captured outcome, not history visibility alone. A complete run can coexist with current target attention. Relevant history includes superseded and withdrawn records; when a target disappears, each stream determines period relevance independently from its retained history. Neither a later timestamp nor an unrelated replay proves coverage ([T09 coverage decision](https://github.com/taxmaxi/taxmaxi/issues/150#issuecomment-5560577947)).

Acceptance and its exact source work commit atomically. The response retains that committed context and job identity; dispatch then enqueues only the exact ready pending job in its recorded mode. A pending sync remains sync even when a correction requested follow-up replay. Dispatch failure preserves acceptance and durable work for existing recovery. Replay completion uses the existing principal recomputation path, including later affected tax years ([T10 dispatch decision](https://github.com/taxmaxi/taxmaxi/issues/150#issuecomment-5561058131)).

Transaction discovery follows current recorded transaction, fee, transfer, and reconciliation links. Disappeared targets remain readable by stable target ID; mutations return `target_unavailable`. There is no universal retained association for discovering a target through a former transaction. Such historical discovery requires a separately recorded writer fact, never matching by old row shape.

## Considered options

Direct tax-result replacements were excluded: the confirmed product scope is to correct inputs and let the engine derive the result. Reusing an asset-wide target for a transaction price was rejected because it would affect unrelated occurrences. Attaching corrections to regenerated leg IDs or reconstructing movement identity during reads was rejected because replay can change those rows. A parallel replay or calculation scheduler was rejected because the existing lifecycle already owns retries, follow-ups, and run coverage.
